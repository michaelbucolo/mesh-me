# mesh.me Public Launch Master Checklist

This document is the single source of truth for production launch readiness. It is intentionally exhaustive so every owner can execute a safe, measurable, and repeatable go-live.

## How to use this checklist

1. Assign each line item to an owner.
2. Add evidence links in your internal tracker.
3. Mark status as `Not Started`, `In Progress`, `Blocked`, or `Done`.
4. Do not launch until all P0 and P1 items are completed.
5. Run the launch rehearsal twice (T-7 days and T-1 day).

## Severity definition

- **P0**: Must be complete before launch. Launch blocker.
- **P1**: High priority. Complete before launch unless formally waived.
- **P2**: Important but can be completed right after launch with owner sign-off.
