# Mesh.me — Complete Product Vision

> This document is the **canonical north star** for Mesh.me. Every person or AI
> agent building the platform should treat it as the source of truth for what
> Mesh.me is, why it exists, how it should work, how it should feel, and the
> standard the finished product must meet.
>
> This is **not** a spec for an MVP or an early prototype. The entire site is
> designed and built around the complete vision from the beginning. When a
> decision is ambiguous, choose the option that moves the real product closer to
> everything described here — and never contradicts it.

---

## 1. The Ultimate Goal

Mesh.me is meant to become **the most used, most preferred, and most popular
social media platform ever created** — a legitimate platform that competes with
and improves upon the largest social networks in the world. Every decision is
made with that level of ambition. The goal is not "functional"; it is **the best
social media platform on the planet.**

That requires deeply analyzing existing platforms and interfaces, what users
love and hate, recommendation algorithms, usability, privacy, AI concerns,
creator needs, cross-platform communication, discovery, animation, device
performance, social habits, why people abandon apps, and why shared posts and
messages go unseen. Mesh.me should solve problems existing platforms have not.

## 2. The Basic Concept

Mesh.me combines a person's **entire social and digital life into one platform.**
Instead of hopping between YouTube, Instagram, Snapchat, Threads, Facebook, X,
and others, users view, manage, and interact with all of them through one
unified experience. The long-term ideal: a person could **delete the other apps
from their phone and keep using those services through Mesh.me.**

Mesh.me is the central place where users explore content, communicate, view
connected accounts, manage their digital presence, post, share, discover,
interact, review analytics, see their relationships visualized, and represent
themselves online.

But Mesh.me is **not** just a combined feed. Its central experience is a
**literal, live, shared visual web called the Mesh.**

## 3. Domain & Initial Entry

The platform lives at **mesh.me**. Visiting it lands on a simple, direct login.

The sequence:

1. The user enters their **username**.
2. They press Enter / continue.
3. Mesh.me checks whether the account exists.
4. If it exists, **that user's Meshi appears.**
5. Meshi acknowledges the username.
6. Meshi asks for the password.
7. The user enters it and presses Enter.
8. After authentication, Meshi reveals the next experience.
9. The user enters the Mesh.

For privacy, Meshi greets the user by **username, never their real name.** The
correct Meshi appearing also confirms the user reached the right account. The
transition from login into the Mesh is **animated**, never a hard page load.

## 4. Meshi

Meshi is central to Mesh.me — **not a logo or decorative mascot. Meshi represents
the user.** Every user has their own Meshi, tied to their identity throughout the
platform. Others recognize a person by their Meshi, and profiles display it so
people know how to identify that user inside the Mesh.

Meshi is **customizable**, so it represents the individual and makes interacting
more personal and fun. Meshi stays relevant across the **entire** experience —
exploring the Mesh, viewing posts, interacting, moving between areas, using Flow,
visiting another person's Mesh, and performing visible social actions. Meshi is
the user's **live representative** inside the platform.

## 5. Meshi as the User's Live Presence

Mesh.me should feel like a **live multiplayer social experience.** As a user
explores, their Meshi represents what they're doing; others present in that area
may see the Meshi moving, viewing, exploring, or interacting. This brings the
feeling of a physical social environment into a digital one — you can see where
people are, what they're paying attention to, and how they react.

Example: when a user likes a post, someone nearby may see that user's Meshi
**throw a small heart toward the post**, and that heart contributes to the like
count. An ordinary button press becomes a **visible, social action.** The
platform should contain many such interactions where a normal digital action is
represented physically through Meshi and the Mesh.

## 6. The Mesh

The Mesh is the **main experience and defining feature.** It is a **literal
visual web** of nodes, strings, connections, people, posts, content,
relationships, and interactive things — a visualization of the social internet.
It shows the **connections between everything** rather than a list, grid, or
ordinary home feed, and it should **never be simplified into a conventional
dashboard.**

The Mesh represents a user's own social world, their friends, content, the
connections between content and between people, other users' areas, the broader
surrounding network, and potentially the whole internet as represented through
Mesh.me. The user should feel they're **exploring a connected digital world**,
not navigating separate web pages.

## 7. Entering Another Person's Mesh

Selecting a friend's node **animates the user traveling into that friend's Mesh**
via a smooth zoom/movement, communicating a move from their own area into
another's. The user can always zoom back out and return. Movement is **spatial**
— not clicking a link to load a separate profile page. Another person's Mesh
feels like a connected part of the **same shared world.**

## 8. The Mesh Is Live and Shared

The Mesh is **not** a private simulation generated separately per user. It is one
**live, shared** thing; everyone participates in the **same underlying world**
with consistent locations, connections, physics, and interactions. What a person
is *allowed* to see depends on privacy and visibility, but the Mesh itself is one
shared system. When something happens, it can be represented live; when someone
moves or interacts, that activity appears to others when appropriate and
permitted. This shared quality is what makes Mesh.me social, not merely visual.

## 9. Physics-Based Interaction

**Everything** in the platform is physics-based — not only the Mesh background,
but the entire interaction system. Objects respond with believable movement,
weight, momentum, resistance, acceleration, deceleration, connection, tension,
and settling. Nodes and strings feel physical; dragging, zooming, opening, and
moving between experiences all feel physical. Interactive objects are never flat
or lifeless. Physics makes the platform fun, satisfying, interactive, and
premium — one coherent physical system rather than unrelated per-element
animations.

## 10. Animation & Continuous Navigation

**Everything is animated and smooth**, and the whole platform feels like it
happens on **one page.** Users should rarely feel sent to unrelated pages.
Transitions visually connect where the user came from with where they're going:
a node expanding into content, the camera moving into another person's Mesh, a
post opening from its position in the web, a chat emerging from the existing
interface, zooming back out instead of a traditional Back button, Meshi moving
with the user, and interface elements responding physically when touched.
Animation is not decoration — it's how users understand that everything belongs
to the same connected experience. It should feel fun, cool, interactive, smooth,
premium, and alive.

## 11. Design Direction

The interface is **not futuristic.** It is clean, new, refreshing, familiar
enough to understand instantly, premium, smooth, and simple. It can learn from
successful interfaces — the current Instagram interface can inspire some
familiar formatting and interaction patterns — but Mesh.me should **not just look
like Instagram.** It takes what works and fits it into Mesh.me's own connected,
animated system. Despite combining many services and a live Mesh, it must never
feel overwhelming; it stays easy to understand.

Brand: cool blue / violet / cyan palette. **No OS/emoji glyphs in product UI —
original custom-drawn shapes only.** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere.

## 12. Performance & Frame Rate

Mesh.me targets the **maximum frame rate the device supports** — the highest
humanly possible within the device's capabilities, ProMotion-level where
available. It never needlessly locks to a low frame rate. Animations, physics,
scrolling, zooming, and movement must be extremely responsive. The physics-based
design only works with excellent performance; lag, stutter, delayed input, or
choppy movement would damage the whole experience. **High performance is part of
the product**, not just an optimization.

## 13. Ghost Mode

Users can enter **Ghost Mode** easily. Their Meshi visually **turns into a
ghost**, signaling private browsing, and their activity is **hidden from others**
throughout the Mesh. Because the normal Mesh is a live environment where Meshis
represent actions, users need a clear way to temporarily hide that presence. The
ghost transformation makes the mode obvious and fits the Meshi system naturally.

## 14. MeChat

MeChat is Mesh.me's communication system — a complete messaging experience that
**also combines and synchronizes messages and shared content from connected
accounts.** It exists because people communicate across too many platforms, so a
huge amount of shared content is never seen (e.g., a post sent via Threads to a
friend who no longer opens Threads).

MeChat solves this: if someone sends something through Threads, the recipient
sees it **inside MeChat** when they use Mesh.me — no need to remember which
original app it came from — and they can **respond through MeChat.** The goal is
for Mesh.me to be the **one place** a person's social communication appears.

## 15. Replacing the Need for Separate Apps

The ideal outcome: users can **delete the other social apps** and still use those
services through Mesh.me. Mesh.me reduces the need to open many feeds, check many
inboxes, remember which app a friend uses, switch apps to respond, maintain a
fragmented identity, or manage content separately per platform. Unifying the
person's social internet is a core purpose.

## 16. Connected Accounts

Users connect existing third-party accounts, letting Mesh.me bring their content,
communication, and digital footprint into one experience. Connected accounts feed
MeChat, the user's Mesh, their profile, Explore, Flow, cross-platform
interaction, creator analytics, and cross-platform posting/management. A person's
Mesh.me account becomes the **center of all their connected identities.** These
connections matter for everyone and are **especially valuable for creators.**

## 17. Users Without Existing Social Media

People **do not need existing social accounts** to use Mesh.me. A new user can
create an account and begin exploring, seeing posts that originate from YouTube,
X, Instagram, and other connected platforms **without** holding an account on
every original service. This makes Mesh.me useful even to people who don't
consider themselves social media users.

## 18. Interacting With Content From a Platform the User Doesn't Have

A person without a given third-party account can still browse that service's
content through Mesh.me. When they try to **interact**, Mesh.me asks them to
connect the relevant account, offering account creation if they don't have one.
Example: a user with no YouTube account encounters a YouTube post, tries to like
it, and Mesh.me prompts them to connect (or create) a YouTube account. Discovery
is never blocked merely because the user hasn't created an account elsewhere.

## 19. Private Third-Party Account Creation

When a user needs a third-party account they don't have, Mesh.me offers two
choices: (1) create it through Mesh.me via a **privacy-focused process**, or (2)
create it manually on the third-party service. The Mesh.me option works like
privacy-oriented account features associated with iCloud: the user's **real email
is hidden** where possible and the **password is encrypted**, designed to stay
secure even if the third-party service suffers a data leak. The user stays in
control and may always choose the manual option.

## 20. Flow

Flow is Mesh.me's short-form experience. Its vertical interaction is familiar to
anyone who uses TikTok, Reels, or Shorts: move vertically through recommended
content. The **vertical direction is the regular recommendation algorithm**,
which should be at least as good as Instagram's. Flow also introduces a separate
**horizontal** interaction.

## 21. Horizontal Context in Flow

Swiping **left/right** shows content **closely related to the current post** —
not necessarily from the same creator, but connected to the same subject, event,
clip, or discussion. For a sports replay, horizontal swipes might show the same
play from another angle, another recording of the event, a news report, an
explainer, related commentary, or another person's perspective. Horizontal =
**explore the context around a post.** Resuming vertical swiping returns to normal
discovery. Two dimensions: **vertical for continued discovery, horizontal for
closely related content.** This requires substantial recommendation/relationship
logic so related posts are *truly* connected.

## 22. Meshi's Presence While Using Flow

Flow does **not** remove the user from the live Mesh. Even while viewing
short-form content, the user's Meshi still appears within the Mesh in relation to
the posts they're viewing, and others can see that Meshi interacting when
visible. Flow is another way of moving through the **same shared world**, not a
disconnected app-within-the-app.

## 23. User Profiles

A profile uses a familiar interface (Facebook-style organization is a fine
inspiration because people already understand it), but a Mesh.me profile is far
broader: it represents the person's **entire digital footprint.** A user sees
content from all connected accounts in one place — YouTube, Instagram, Snapchat,
other posts, Mesh.me activity, connected presence — presented through Mesh.me's
**own** interface rather than copying another platform. The user's **Meshi** is
visible so visitors recognize how they appear throughout the live Mesh.

## 24. The Mesh as a Shareable Representation of the User

A person's Mesh **represents them** and is **shareable.** It is more than a
profile page — it represents their connections, content, interactions, social
presence, digital footprint, and activity throughout the connected world. Sharing
someone's Mesh lets others **experience a visual representation of that person**
rather than a static list of posts.

## 25. Explore

Explore lets users **discover** content (YouTube's Explore is a fine inspiration
for discovery patterns), but Mesh.me Explore **combines content across multiple
platforms**, not just native Mesh.me content. It should be useful even if the
user hasn't connected every outside platform.

## 26. Creator Use

Mesh.me gives creators major advantages. Instead of managing presence separately
across platforms, creators manage their entire footprint in one place, use
connected accounts for **mass posting / cross-platform distribution**, and get
**combined analytics** no single platform provides in one view: how people found
them, which platform is winning, which post performed best per platform, why a
post did better on one platform, how performance differs across platforms, and
how content moves through their audience and connected accounts. The analytics
**reveal relationships invisible** when each platform reports separately.

## 27. Privacy

**User privacy is the number-one priority.** Mesh.me is as encrypted as possible.
Users have privacy from **other users and from Mesh.me itself.** Privacy is
considered across accounts, connected services, login, messages, profile
visibility, live presence, Ghost Mode, third-party account creation, synced
content, stored information, and user activity. The Mesh being live must **never**
eliminate a person's ability to control their privacy.

## 28. Zero AI-Generated Content

Mesh.me has **zero AI-generated content.** AI-generated posts are not part of the
platform; the visible content people consume is not filled with AI material. This
is a **firm rule.** The platform centers real people and human-created content.

## 29. The Only User-Facing AI

The **only** user-facing AI is **Meshi.** Users don't encounter many visible AI
systems, accounts, or personalities. Meshi is the sole AI that interacts directly
with the user, keeping the experience understandable and consistent — and
separate from the rule against AI-generated public content. Meshi can be the
platform's user-facing intelligence without AI-generated posts entering the
social content.

## 30. AI-Based Moderation Before Publication

Although AI-generated public content is disallowed, Mesh.me uses AI **behind the
scenes for moderation.** Before content becomes visible or is officially posted —
whether posted directly through Mesh.me, synced in, or otherwise becoming visible
— it is checked by **multiple AI moderation systems** to catch problems before
other users encounter it. AI has an important **private moderation role** even
though it must not create public social content.

## 31. Zero Advertisements

Mesh.me contains **zero advertisements.** Advertising is not part of the platform
and the business never depends on inserting ads into the Mesh, Flow, MeChat,
profiles, Explore, creator content, or any other experience. **Firm rule.**

## 32. MeshPro as the Only Revenue Source

Mesh.me makes money **only** through **MeshPro subscriptions** — the sole
revenue source. No additional revenue models that undermine the experience.

## 33. The Free Experience

People can use the platform essentially to its **fullest potential without
paying.** The normal experience stays **complete.** Important functionality is
never removed to force subscriptions. Free users meaningfully use the Mesh,
Meshi, Flow, MeChat, profiles, Explore, connected experiences, core privacy
features, and the overall platform. MeshPro must never make the free version
feel broken or deliberately incomplete.

## 34. MeshPro's Required Value

Even though free stays complete, MeshPro must provide enough value that people
**genuinely want** it — because of how useful it is, not because free was
damaged. It should feel like a good, cost-efficient deal worth more than its
price, valuable to creators, ordinary users, and anyone (not one narrow
audience), and **especially** valuable to creators given their cross-platform
management and analytics needs. It adds meaningful capabilities without taking
from the main platform.

## 35. Monthly Trails

At the end of every month, Mesh.me generates a **Trail** — like Spotify Wrapped,
but for the user's month on Mesh.me. It shows the **literal path the user
traveled through the Mesh**: the connections they interacted with, viewed,
explored, and spent time around. Because the Mesh is nodes, strings, and
connections, the recap appears as a **literal trail** through those relationships
— making the month feel like a **journey through the Mesh**, not a pile of stats.

## 36. People Who Do Not Use Social Media

Mesh.me is not only for heavy social media users. It **makes sense as a
standalone experience.** Existing accounts expand it but are never mandatory for
value. A new user can create a Mesh.me identity, have a Meshi, explore the Mesh,
discover content, communicate, and build connections **without** already having
accounts everywhere. Mesh.me is for **everyone and anyone.**

## 37. Research Expectations

Mesh.me is designed with **deep research, not assumptions.** The process analyzes
social platforms and UI patterns, the latest Instagram interface, algorithms,
what works and what users complain about, why people leave, what people dislike
about AI and about social media, why content sharing fails, what creators /
everyday users / non-users need, what's uniquely possible for Mesh.me, how a
shared social world operates, how physics and live presence improve interaction,
how privacy coexists with visible activity, and how a subscription stays valuable
without reducing the free platform. The product is repeatedly analyzed, improved,
and double- and triple-checked. Nothing is treated casually.

## 38. Final Product Standard

Mesh.me is delivered as a **complete product**, not framed around a minimal
version first. Every major system is planned as part of the final platform: the
login experience, Meshi, the shared live Mesh, nodes and strings, physics,
animation, Ghost Mode, MeChat, connected accounts, Flow, horizontal related
content, profiles, Explore, third-party account interaction, private account
creation, creator management, creator analytics, human-only content, AI
moderation, privacy, maximum device performance, monthly Trails, MeshPro, and
zero advertisements. It must feel complete, polished, interactive, and premium.

**The standard is simple: Mesh.me is meant to become the social platform people
prefer over every alternative. Every part is designed with that outcome in mind.**
