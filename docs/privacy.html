Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 5, 2026

1. Data we store

  • Account data (username, email, password hash).
  • Connections (which services you connect and the access tokens required to call their APIs).
  • Cached feed items to make your feed fast.
  • Notification rules and notification events.
  • Device tokens if you enable push notifications.

2. How we use your data

We use your data to provide the MeshMe experience: fetching feeds, rendering your mesh, and delivering notifications you configure.

3. Tokens

OAuth tokens allow MeshMe to read and act on your behalf when you click actions. Keep your instance secure. In this open-source demo build, tokens are stored in the database. For production you should encrypt tokens at rest.

4. Third parties

Connected Services have their own policies. When MeshMe calls their APIs, it sends requests that may include identifiers required by those services.

5. Deleting your data

You can disconnect services and delete your account (feature can be added) to remove stored data.
