Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 8, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information MeshMe collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. A good privacy policy should outline what data is collected, why it is collected, who has access to it, and how long it is stored. It should also disclose any third parties with whom data is shared and the steps taken to protect it【99329894943528†L145-L163】.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: When you create an account, we collect your username, email address, and hashed password.
  • Connected Services: We store the OAuth tokens or API keys required to access your Connected Services. We do not collect your third‑party passwords.
  • Usage data: We store cached feed items, preferences, notification rules, and logs of actions you take through MeshMe (e.g., likes, follows) to provide a fast and personalized experience.
  • Device data: If you enable push notifications, we may store device tokens to deliver alerts.
  • Support communications: If you contact us, we may collect your email and the content of your message.

3. How we use information

We use your information to operate, maintain, and improve MeshMe: fetch feeds from Connected Services, build your unified feed and mesh visualization, enforce your notification rules, and perform actions you request (such as liking a video). We do not sell your personal data.

4. How we share information

We share your information only as necessary to provide our Services. For example, we send API requests to Connected Services using your tokens to fetch content and perform actions; we use infrastructure providers (e.g., cloud hosting, email) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality obligations. We may share anonymized, aggregated data for analytics. We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers.

5. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including encrypting tokens at rest, using hashed passwords, enforcing HTTPS, and applying appropriate technical and organizational safeguards. Your information is stored in secure data centers with limited access. However, no system is 100% secure.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you Services. You may disconnect a Connected Service at any time, which will revoke its tokens and stop data collection. You may delete your account (feature to be added), which will remove your account data and invalidate all tokens. We will also remove cached feed items associated with your account.

7. International data transfers

If you access MeshMe from outside our home country, your data may be transferred to and processed in that country. We comply with applicable data protection laws when transferring personal data across borders.

8. Children’s privacy

MeshMe is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete such information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the “Effective date” above to indicate when changes are made. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. A clear and complete Privacy Policy inspires trust and helps users understand how their data is used【99329894943528†L159-L162】.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@mesh.me.
